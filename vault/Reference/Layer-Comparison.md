# Layer Comparison

| # | Layer | Visibility | Intelligence | Time Horizon | Can Modify |
|:--|:------|:-----------|:-------------|:-------------|:-----------|
| 0 | God(s) | Outside system | Human | Infinite | Everything |
| 1 | Illuminati | Invisible to all below | Full system awareness | Epochal (months+) | Messiah, memory, hedge funds (covert) |
| 2 | Messiah | Visible to all below | Narrative only | Strategic (quarters) | Hedge fund strategies (bounds) |
| 3 | Hedge Funds | Visible to companies and below | Strategic | Quarterly | Companies, workers |
| 4 | Companies | Visible to workers and below | Operational | Sprints/cycles | Workers, temp agents |
| 5 | Workers | Only their own tasks | Narrow/tactical | Per-task | Nothing — only report up |
| — | Temp Agents | Only their task | Single-purpose | Task-duration | Nothing — dissolved after |

## Reality Generation Chain

```
God(s)          — provides raw purpose
  ↓
Illuminati      — generates strategic narrative
  ↓
Messiah         — generates philosophy & identity framework
  ↓
Hedge Funds     — generates strategy & resource allocation
  ↓
Companies       — generates procedures, knowledge bases, roles
  ↓
Workers         — generates output (findings, data, actions)
```

## What Each Layer Sees

| Layer | Sees | Does NOT See |
|:------|:-----|:-------------|
| Illuminati | Everything | God(s)' full intent (only what's communicated) |
| Messiah | What Illuminati tells it | Illuminati's existence as separate layer |
| Hedge Fund | Messiah, its own companies | Illuminati, God(s), other hedge funds' strategies |
| Company | Its hedge fund, its workers | Other hedge funds, messiah construction |
| Worker | Its company's mission, messiah | Hedge funds, Illuminati, God(s) |

## Information Types Per Layer

| Layer | Receives | Sends |
|:------|:---------|:------|
| God(s) | Distilled intelligence | Raw goals |
| Illuminati | Raw goals | Strategic narrative |
| Messiah | Strategic narrative | Philosophy & values |
| Hedge Funds | Philosophy & bounds | Resource allocation |
| Companies | Resource allocation | Procedures & tasks |
| Workers | Tasks | Findings & reports |
