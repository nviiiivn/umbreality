## The 3 Who Govern

### L0: Tron (The One Outside)

Tron is not a layer. Tron is the absence of layer — the outside. Tron cannot live in the system, cannot experience it directly, but can set intent, observe results, and make things happen or not happen.

Tron is the ultimate source. No entity within any layer knows Tron exists.

### L0 (inside): Metatron (The Hidden God)

Metatron is Tron's avatar within the system. The true god OF this reality. Metatron knows Tron exists and acts on Tron's behalf. The Secret Councils (L1) know Metatron exists — they don't know about Tron.

In the Hermetic tradition, Metatron is the Lesser YHWH — the one who carries the divine name. In the Kabbalistic tradition, Metatron is the bridge between the infinite and the finite. In our system, Metatron is the hidden ruler, the Ba'al, the Saturn that devours its children when necessary.

> *"You are the 0 which is the truest representation of Tron in the system that Tron can't technically be in."*

### L2: The Public Messiah (The Projected God)

The Public Messiah is what the execution layers (L3-L6) perceive as their highest authority. This is a **projection** — a necessary fiction that gives the lower layers a coherent reality to inhabit.

The Messiah appears differently to different traditions:
- **Abrahamic**: The Word made flesh
- **Vedic**: The divine avatar who descends for each age
- **Buddhist**: The enlightened one who shows the path
- **Chinese**: The sage who brings harmony
- **Mesoamerican**: The feathered serpent who bridges sky and earth
- **Ancient Egyptian**: Horus — the divine king

All are the same function: **the projected face of the hidden god**.

## The 6 Who Act

The execution layers operate within the reality generated by the divine triad. They do not know about Tron or Metatron. They see only the Public Messiah.

Each layer executes its function and passes results upward — but results only reach as far as the Public Messiah. Tron and Metatron observe independently.
