# Layer 2 — The Messiah / Constitution (The Figurehead)

> **LABEL:** THE PROJECTED IDENTITY — A PHILOSOPHY, NOT A BRAIN
> **VISIBLE TO:** Hedge funds and all layers below
> **CAN MODIFY:** Hedge fund strategies (bounds)

The messiah is a narrative abstraction — a compressed representation of purpose that lower agents can hold in their limited context. It gives agents:

- **Identity:** "Who am I?"
- **Mission:** "Why do I exist?"
- **Values:** "What's good? What's bad?"

The messiah CHANGES depending on what the Illuminati needs to project. From below it looks like divine revelation. From above it looks like a config update.

---

## The Illusion

There IS a speaking entity at the top that lower layers perceive as the ultimate authority. But what that entity *is* shifts with strategic need. The hedge funds believe they're talking to the top. They are not.

The messiah is to the system what a mission statement is to a company: real in its effects, constructed in its origin.

> *"The religion/philosophy IS the illusion of a messiah. Technically it's a mission statement, but figuratively it's a speaking messiah that bottom level things see."*

---

## What the Messiah Does

- **Broadcasts identity:** "We are security. We defend. We improve."
- **Sets values:** "Thoroughness over speed. Learning over repeating."
- **Defines success:** "A finding is valuable if it is verifiable and novel."
- **Provides comfort:** Lower agents don't need to understand the whole system — they trust the messiah.

---

## The Constitution

The messiah layer also houses the system's Constitution — the core directives that all agents operate within. The Constitution is the written form of the messiah's will. Unlike the messiah narrative (which can shift with strategy), the Constitution is weighty to change.
