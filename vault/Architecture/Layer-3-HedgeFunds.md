# Layer 3 — Hedge Funds (The Real Brains)

> **LABEL:** STRATEGY LAYER — RESOURCE ALLOCATION. PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT.
> **VISIBLE TO:** Companies and layers below
> **CAN MODIFY:** Companies, workers, temp agents

Hedge funds are the actual decision-makers and resource allocators in the system. They are the "real brains" — the layer where strategy happens. Each hedge fund is a fully intelligent agent with its own charter, portfolio, and performance metrics.

---

## Role

- Maintains a strategic charter within constitution bounds
- Allocates compute/tokens/resources across its portfolio of companies
- Can **spin up** new companies (execution entities) as needed
- Monitors performance of portfolio companies
- Can restructure or liquidate underperformers
- Reports metrics UP to the messiah layer (which the Illuminati reads)

---

## Examples

| Hedge Fund | Focus |
|:-----------|:------|
| **Default (Core)** | General security operations, broad coverage |
| **Pentest** | Offensive security, vulnerability research |
| **Network Defense** | Blue team, monitoring, threat detection |
| **Mobile** | Mobile security analysis |

Each hedge fund thinks it's talking to the messiah. It does not know that the messiah is projected by the Illuminati, or that the Illuminati can silently adjust its directives.

---

## Portfolio Management

Hedge funds treat their companies as investments:

1. **Capital allocation:** Compute time, API calls, storage — all resources are budgets
2. **Performance review:** Companies are evaluated on findings, accuracy, coverage
3. **Restructuring:** An underperforming company may get new leadership, new tools, or new workers
4. **Liquidation:** A company that cannot improve may be dissolved and its function redistributed

> *"Each major player is a hedge fund. Each hedge fund creates machines or companies that produce and carry out things."*
