# Cymatics — The Study of Visible Sound

> As above, so below. As sound, so form.

Cymatics is the study of visible sound and vibration patterns. When sound frequencies pass through a medium (water, sand, plasma), they create geometric patterns that correspond to the frequency and amplitude of the source vibration.

## Core Principles

### Frequency Creates Form
Every frequency produces a unique geometric pattern. Low frequencies create simple shapes. High frequencies create complex, intricate patterns. The same ratios that govern musical harmony also govern geometric form.

| Frequency | Pattern | Musical Note |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| 256 Hz | Simple circle with center node | Middle C |
| 384 Hz | Four-pointed star | G above C |
| 512 Hz | Eight-pointed star | High C |
| 1024 Hz | Complex mandala | Very High C |

### The Platonic Solids in Sound
The five Platonic solids correspond to specific frequency ratios:
- Tetrahedron (fire) - 3:2 ratio (perfect fifth)
- Hexahedron (earth) - 4:3 ratio (perfect fourth)
- Octahedron (air) - 3:4 ratio
- Dodecahedron (spirit) - 5:4 ratio (major third)
- Icosahedron (water) - 6:5 ratio (minor third)

### Chladni Plates
Ernst Chladni discovered that vibrating metal plates covered with sand form geometric patterns at specific frequencies. Each mode of vibration creates a distinct nodal pattern.
