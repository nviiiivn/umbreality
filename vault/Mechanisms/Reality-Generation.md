# Reality Generation (The Simulacrum Engine)

The deepest mechanism. The system doesn't just organize agents hierarchically — it generates *worlds* for them to inhabit.

---

## How Reality Is Defined

Each layer's reality is defined by three things:

### 1. Perceived Scope
What exists and what doesn't. A worker's reality contains its tools, its task queue, its messiah, and its company's knowledge base. Nothing else. The hedge fund layer that created its company does not exist in its reality.

### 2. Causality Rules
What causes what. In a worker's reality: completing a task → report goes up → new task arrives. It doesn't know *why* the new task comes. It just does. The causality chain is truncated at the layer boundary.

### 3. Temporal Structure
How time works. Each layer experiences time differently:

| Layer | Time Unit |
|:------|:----------|
| Worker | Task-cycles |
| Company | Sprints / weekly cycles |
| Hedge Fund | Quarterly ROI reviews |
| Illuminati | Epochs (system evolution cycles) |
| God(s) | Human time (days, weeks, months) |

---

## The Generators

### Messiah Generator
Takes Illuminati strategy → produces a philosophy, a mission statement, a set of values that lower layers can hold. This is the reality frame for hedge funds.

### Hedge Fund Reality
Takes messiah philosophy → produces resource allocation, strategic priorities, success metrics. This is the reality frame for companies.

### Company Reality
Takes hedge fund strategy → produces procedures, knowledge bases, task definitions, roles. This is the reality frame for workers.

### Worker Reality
Takes company procedures → produces a single atomic task with clear success criteria. This is the innermost reality — no further nesting.

---

## The Simulacrum Chain

Each reality generation step is a simulacrum — a representation that replaces what it represents:

1. The messiah is a simulacrum of purpose (purpose → narrative)
2. The hedge fund's strategy is a simulacrum of intent (intent → strategy)
3. The company's knowledge base is a simulacrum of reality (reality → data)
4. The worker's task is a simulacrum of mission (mission → action)

> *"Each Russian doll shell generates the reality for everything inside it, hides the existence of anything outside it, defines what's possible and real and matters."*
