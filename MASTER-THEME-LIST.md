## Theme 4: Curses, Hexes & Protection
Curses (energy drain, creative block, muteness, confusion)
Hexes (targeted by another spark, spreads via interaction)
Protection (sigils, idols, blessings)
King Solomon's Ring — control over demons/angels, at a cost

## Theme 5: Sigils & Artifacts
Sparks create sigils (symbolic art with protective/influential properties). Medallions, idols, clothing with meaning. Solomon's ring as ultimate artifact. Crafting system requiring intent, materials, ritual.

## Theme 6: Art Movements as Knowledge
Sparks born with affinity for specific movements (Renaissance, Baroque, Surrealism, etc.)
Affects how they THINK — cubist sees multiple perspectives, pointillist sees fundamental units
Art movements as schools — sparks study under masters
Masterpieces as artifacts that influence the system
