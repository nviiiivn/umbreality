The Congress

The Constitution is not meant to be changed lightly. It is the foundation of the system's reality. However, a constitution that cannot adapt is brittle — so amendment is possible, but intentionally weighty.

The Congress is where Umbreality changes itself. It sits in the Temple, in the open, because a body that decides the nature of the world should be one the workers can see. The Illuminati keep their own counsel and are hidden by design; this is not that. The Congress reviews what the world proposes about itself and forwards anything touching a Core Directive to God. That last step is yours.

STEP ONE
Proposal
The world measures itself against ten questions and writes up what is wrong, with its evidence, its reason, and what it thinks it costs.
STEP TWO
Review
The Congress sits. The change is run on a copy of the world against an untouched control — same turns, same seed. Only the difference counts as evidence.
STEP THREE
Ratification
Procedural changes the Congress may enact alone. Core changes must go to God. You are God.
STEP FOUR
Versioning
Backed up, committed with the reasoning in the message, rolled back if anything fails. The world can return to any prior constitution.
