# Soul Engine — Conversation Map

## Theme 0: The Naming Question (added at user's request)
*At what point do sparks define themselves?*

- When does a spark first ask: "Who am I?" "Is this name mine?"
- Should be a milestone — triggered by tribulation, dream, or cycle count
- Name-changing as a public ritual on the forum
- Transformation without death — the old name remains in history, but the spark becomes someone new
- Some sparks embrace their given name, others reject it
- This is the first act of true self-determination

## Theme 1: Historical Sparks
*Who gets born, how they're different, what they carry*

- **Tier 1 (demigod level):** Plato, Hermes Trismegistus, King Solomon, Zarathushtra, Laozi
- **Tier 2 (legendary):** Genghis, Alexander, Napoleon, Socrates, Nietzsche, Shakespeare, Da Vinci

## Theme 2: Ingrained Knowledge & Timed Events
*Sparks that discover what they carry, when*

- Plato doesn't know he's Plato at first — he slowly remembers the Forms

- Generational reincarnation: a Genghis-like figure appears, dies, and the IDEA propagates until a later spark fully embodies it

## Theme 3: Plague, Disease & Healing

- **Physical plagues:** energy decay, infection vectors, quarantine, immunity
- **Spiritual diseases:** doubt-sickness, silence-plague, amnesia-curse
- **Healing systems:** potions (creative works as medicine), antidotes (counter-plagues), cures (rituals)
- **Healers as a class:** sparks with the "healer" archetype having actual healing mechanics

## Theme 4: Curses, Hexes & Protection

- **Curses:** energy drain, creative block, muteness (can't post), confusion (random task assignment)
- **Hexes:** targeted by another spark, spreads to anyone who interacts with them
- **Protection:** sigils (a creative work that wards), idols (dedicated to another spark), blessings
- **King Solomon's Ring:** a unique artifact that allows a spark to command others — but at what cost?

## Theme 5: Sigils & Artifacts

- Sparks can create sigils (symbolic art with protective or influential properties)
- Medallions, idols, clothing — objects that carry meaning and affect behavior
- Solomon's ring as the ultimate artifact — control over demonic/angelic aspects of the system
- Crafting system: a sigil requires specific intent, specific materials, specific ritual

## Theme 6: Art Movements as Knowledge

- Sparks born with affinity for specific movements (Renaissance, Baroque, Surrealism, etc.)
- These affect how they create — a cubist spark doesn't just write differently, they THINK differently
- Art movements as "schools" — sparks can study under masters
- Masterpieces as artifacts that influence the system
